# Target-definition gate

The independently verified Release uses `lane-observed-confirmation-v1`, here called **`recovery_confirmation_v1`** for reporting. The observed unit is `(detector_id, direction, lane_id)`, *not* a verified detector or road segment. One event follows two consecutive usable bins at speed/baseline ≤0.5. Prediction is at availability of the second low bin (end +10 min). A normal reference is the January-2023 lane 85th percentile of eligible positive-flow 5-minute speed bins. Three consecutive usable high bins at ratio ≥0.8 witness recovery confirmation; the label is positive only if the third bin ends within the horizon. Missing usable follow-up yields unknown, not negative. See `docs/data_contract.md` §3 for the frozen rule, 60-minute separation, censoring, prediction-time lag and 60-minute split purging. All current 96-lane metrics report this confirmation target **only**.

The Proposal review describes *recovery start within 30 minutes with subsequent 10-minute persistence*. This is **not equivalent** to confirmation by 30 minutes. Start at 29 min could require verifiable observations through at least 39 min; the time grid and availability policies may require further follow-up. The first high observation must be checked against the actual uninterrupted source bins, not obtained by subtracting 10 minutes from a published confirmation time. Unknown follow-up must remain censored. An event at a train/validation boundary also needs purging for the full horizon **plus** sustained follow-up (at least 40 minutes for a 30-minute start target), and any prediction-time lag must be included. The current `events.parquet` contains outcomes and five-minute bins but the original Proposal PDF/approved definition was not located; **`recovery_start_sustained_v1` is NOT IMPLEMENTED, NOT RUN**. No relabeling of the immutable published Release was attempted. Member 1/4 and team approval required before freezing a second independent label manifest.
